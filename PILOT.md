# Practical employee pilot

Run this pilot with a small volunteer group using their company accounts before wider rollout. This document is a proposed test protocol, not a record of completed tenant tests.

## Participants and tasks

Include colleagues from Finance, HR or Recruitment, Operations, and Reporting or team leadership, with a mix of AI experience. Ask each person to complete one work task and one guided practice exercise. Use the fictional examples first; apply the method to real work only with sources they are permitted to use in approved company tools.

Suggested tasks:

- Finance: invoice-query response and rule-based exception practice.
- HR: employee learning enquiry and source-based answer checking.
- Recruitment: hiring-manager intake and role-linked interview preparation.
- Operations: queue handover and meeting-action confirmation.
- Reporting: formula/denominator check and KPI interpretation.
- Team leaders: meeting preparation and a decision brief.
- One enabled Studio user: the private email-to-notification training flow, including unrelated and incomplete messages.

## Observe the complete workflow

1. State the work need without naming a lesson. Observe whether the colleague finds a relevant task using the home shortcuts or discovery.
2. Ask them to open the correct Google application, choose the right source, and use the work prompt without coaching.
3. Have them check the result against the original and correct it before considering it usable.
4. Ask them to save the activity, mark Used in my work where applicable, download the job aid, and find it again later.
5. For practice, check that they attempt the exercise before revealing the worked example.
6. For Studio, inspect the actual starter, variables, recipients, run history, every route, and switch off the training flow afterwards. Test runs take real actions.

Record actual observations only. If comparing time, obtain an equivalent baseline and include review/corrections. Record setup and maintenance separately. Do not turn a few observations into company ROI, cash savings or employee rankings.

## Decide what to improve

Investigate any task that needs coaching to start, points to an unavailable feature without a useful fallback, produces an unclear deliverable, or makes source checking difficult. Confirm that a colleague can repeat the workflow the next day using the job aid. Recheck Gems/Skills availability and the company account's app names before recording screenshots or sharing instructions.

No employee work documents, messages or pilot results need to be uploaded to this learning site. Record pilot observations through the company's existing approved process.
